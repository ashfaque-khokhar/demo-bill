Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about online invoice creation and GST billing.

What is an online GST invoice generator?

An online GST invoice generator is a tool that helps a business prepare an invoice with seller and buyer details, item descriptions, taxable values and applicable GST calculations.

Can I calculate CGST, SGST and IGST?

Bill APP is planned to support CGST and SGST for eligible intra-state transactions and IGST for eligible inter-state transactions. Always verify the place of supply, tax rate and invoice details before issuing a tax invoice.

Can I download an invoice as a PDF?

PDF export is part of the Bill APP requirements. Once the application is deployed, users will be able to prepare a document and download its PDF.

Can I create invoices with discounts?

The planned billing workflow supports both fixed-amount and percentage-based discounts. The final invoice should clearly show how the discount is applied.

Which invoice paper sizes are supported?

The planned layouts include A4 and A5 page sizes. Check the exported document before printing to ensure it matches your business requirements.

Does generating an invoice make it a government e-invoice?

No. Creating or downloading a PDF invoice does not by itself register an e-invoice with the GST Invoice Registration Portal or generate an Invoice Reference Number (IRN). If e-invoicing applies to your business, complete the required process through an authorized system.

Is every invoice generated with this tool automatically GST compliant?

No software can guarantee compliance from incomplete or incorrect inputs. Businesses should verify GST registration details, invoice particulars, tax rates, place of supply and applicable legal requirements. Consult a qualified tax professional when needed.
