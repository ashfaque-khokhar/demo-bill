Saved locally in this browser. Changing settings does not alter previously saved documents. Tap a section to expand or collapse.

  • Appearance — Theme applies immediately (app colors only; preview paper stays white). Paper size sets preview, print, and PDF layout — save settings after changing it.
  • Business profile — Shown as your name and address on new invoices.
  • Contact & web — Optional details for customers to reach you.
  • Tax & GST — Used for GST invoices and place-of-supply rules.
  • Payment & footer — Payment lines and terms printed at the bottom of invoices.
  • Logo & branding — Appears in the invoice header when uploaded.
  • Invoice numbering — How new invoice numbers are generated. Tokens: {PREFIX} {YYYY} {YY} {MM} {SEQ}. Separate sequence per document type.
  • Table column labels — Custom wording for line items and totals on the invoice.
  • Item catalogue — Quick-pick items when adding rows in the editor.
